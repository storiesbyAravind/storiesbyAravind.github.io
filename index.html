Meet The Storyteller

More Than Poses—I Freeze The Feeling Inside The Moment.

Namaskara! I'm Arvind from Hospet. Over the past 5+ years of documenting weddings and traditional family ceremonies, I realized that the most unforgettable photographs aren't stiff studio poses—they are a father's quiet tear during Kanyaadhaan, a mother's glowing smile during her Seemantham, and the joyful laughter of a child's first birthday.

Whether we are shooting a grand wedding mantapa, a sacred Gruhapravesha (House Warming) pooja at dawn, or a cinematic pre-wedding amidst the ancient ruins of Hampi, I blend into your family so everyone feels relaxed, natural, and truly themselves.
